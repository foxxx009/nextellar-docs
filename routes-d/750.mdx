# Progressive Decentralization Guide

This guide details a comprehensive plan for progressive decentralization of a Stellar dApp.

## Decentralization Phases
1. Incubation: Core team makes fast iterations.
2. Community Handover: Introducing governance tokens.
3. Autonomous Protocol: True DAO controls contract upgrades.

## Governance Handoff
As the community scales, the multi-sig treasury expands to include key community actors.

## Example Roadmap
- Q1: Token launch
- Q2: Staking rewards
- Q3: Governance voting enabled
- Q4: Protocol parameters controlled by DAO
